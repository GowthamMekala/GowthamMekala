## About

Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate building practical capability in **systems engineering, electrical design and EV technology**. My academic foundation in electrical systems, electronics and control concepts is applied through project-based work that treats power, control and communication as one integrated system.

My main portfolio project is a conceptual, system-level design of a **Solar PV-powered EV charging system**, taking a design from requirements and architecture through electrical calculations, CAD drawings, schedules and verification documentation.

I am targeting graduate and entry-level roles in **systems, electrical design, EV and vehicle systems, and CAD / AutoCAD design engineering**.
