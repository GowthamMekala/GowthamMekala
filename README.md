# 🧩 Engineering Approach

### 01 — Understand

Identify the engineering problem, requirements, constraints, functions, and interfaces.

### 02 — Architect

Define how electrical, electronic, power, control, and vehicle subsystems interact.

### 03 — Design

Develop structured **CAD layouts, electrical diagrams, system architectures, and technical designs**.

### 04 — Document

Communicate engineering intent through **drawings, documentation, interfaces, schedules, and design records**.

### 05 — Verify

Review the design against requirements and identify areas requiring further engineering validation.
